Field study (brand imagery)

No radiographs as decoration. No stock photography. Compositions are drawn from the geometry of imaging.

What Bonakala never looks like

  • No purple-to-blue gradients, glass blur cards, floating 3D blobs, or hero robots.
  • No AI-generated imagery of people. No stock doctors pointing at screens.
  • No emoji in product UI. No exclamation marks. No copy that starts with "Welcome to".
  • No invented statistics, testimonials or partner logos in demos.
  • No AI output styled like human-entered data.
