# 18 — Competitive Differentiation: Why Bonakala Beats the Market

## 1. The market's standard stack
A typical SA private radiology group runs a commercial RIS, a separate PACS (often vendor-tied to
modalities), a dictation/reporting tool, a billing/practice-management system connected to a claims
switch, an accounting package, spreadsheets for shareholder distributions, and, increasingly, one or
two bolt-on AI products. Each boundary is a re-key, a reconciliation and a blind spot. Patient
contact is by phone and SMS. Results go to referrers by fax, email or a portal per group.

## 3. Feature completeness commitments
"Fully developed" means each module ships with: all personas' surfaces, exception handling, audit,
analytics, automation levels documented, integration points, and tests. The release plan (19) does
not ship a module in a partial state; a module is either in shadow/beta for a named pilot site or
complete.

## 4. Claims we make and claims we do not
We claim: original brand and design system; full-process coverage; automation with leashes; SA
regulatory alignment; offline continuity; unified finance for subsidiaries and JVs.
We do not claim: that AI diagnoses; specific accuracy numbers before validation on SA data;
regulatory approval before SAHPRA processes are complete; that any tariff or price in this spec is
current.

## 5. Moats over time
1. **Data network effects**: the largest longitudinal imaging + outcomes + billing dataset in SA under
   lawful governance → better models, better benchmarks, better contracts with funders.
2. **Referrer lock-in through service**: fastest reports, closed-loop critical results, integration
   into their systems.
3. **Patient loyalty through ownership of their images and results.**
4. **Funder trust**: cleanest claims, audit-ready, appropriateness guidance → preferred DSP status.
5. **Radiologist proposition**: JV equity, the best reading environment, fair reading fees, hub
   flexibility (work from anywhere in SA).
